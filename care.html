alive & fine

don’t read it. do it. your pet stays on screen while you scroll, and every step happens to it for real.

01 / 06 · feed it

−●+
  1. 01

    feed it −

    press the left key. a little snack drops in and gets eaten in four bites. one snack fills one block of full. four is the max: after that it just looks at you.

  2. 02

    play with it ●

    the middle key throws a ball. it chases it, it hops, it sings. playing fills joy, and sometimes makes it a bit hungry again. that’s fair.

  3. 03

    clean up +

    what goes in comes out, in the bottom-right corner, as (@). the right key sweeps the screen. a clean screen sparkles. a dirty one makes it sick: x.x.

  4. 04

    pet it ☝

    tap the screen. no reason. hearts come out. it doesn’t change any number, it just changes the day.

  5. 05

    let it sleep ☾

    it naps when it wants to. don’t press anything. the lights go down, it does zZ. waking it up makes it >.<.

  6. 06

    or it bites !

    forget it long enough and full hits zero. then it gets angry and starts biting whatever you hover: buttons, photos, this paragraph. feeding it makes it stop. the marks stay.

read the face

it can’t talk. it has twelve faces instead. hover a row and yours makes it.

    still alive? good.

    see what happens if you don’t

    or go back and feed it → home
